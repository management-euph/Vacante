# Codebase Audit — 2026-10-08

**Scope:** the whole repository: `travel_core`, `novoton_holidays`, `sphinx_holidays`, `eurosite`, `fgo_invoicing`, `netopia_payments`, plus `dev/`, `scripts/`, `docker/` and CI. About 250k lines. This is the first audit of `netopia_payments`, `eurosite` and `fgo_invoicing`. Earlier audits left them out.

**How it was done**

1. The full toolchain was run locally (results in [§8](#8-toolchain-and-ci-results)).
2. Thirteen auditors each read one area end to end and reported only what they could tie to specific lines of code.
3. Independent verifiers then tried to refute every finding against the code. Each Critical and High finding had two verifiers: one checked that the code really does what is claimed, the other judged the real-world impact. Medium and Low findings had one skeptic per area. No finding was refuted. 27 were confirmed with corrections, which are folded in below.
4. **Severity is the verified one.** Where verifiers disagreed, the lower severity is shown.
5. Findings reported by more than one auditor are merged, and their IDs are listed.

**What this audit cannot see:** the CS-Cart core (not in the repo), the live provider and payment APIs (no network calls were made), and the production store's settings. Findings that depend on any of these say so. [§10](#10-what-to-confirm-on-the-live-store) lists what to check on the real store.

---

## Summary

The engineering baseline is strong: PHPStan level 10 on an empty baseline, Rector and php-cs-fixer clean, 3,214 PHPUnit tests and 228 Vitest tests passing, TLS and timeouts on almost every HTTP client, parameterised SQL, XXE-safe XML parsing, and atomic invoice claiming in FGO.

The serious problems sit in the parts that were never audited, and at the seams between add-ons. In order of urgency:

1. **Guest bookings are readable and writable by anyone (Critical).** The ownership check treats `user_id = 0` as a match. Every anonymous visitor therefore owns every guest booking. Booking ids are sequential, so travellers' names and children's dates of birth can be read by stepping through them, and Novoton bookings can be rewritten.
2. **Several money paths trust data the browser sent.**
   - Novoton builds the supplier reservation from a hidden `rooms_data` field it never re-prices (Critical).
   - A deposit balance can be marked paid by any order line that carries its id.
   - The Sphinx checkout check never looks at the price the line actually charges.
   - A plain hotel product line silently picks up a full reservation.
3. **Supplier bookings and payments are not idempotent.**
   - Eurosite and Novoton re-send booking requests after timeouts, which can create duplicate reservations.
   - All providers book at order creation, before payment, and never cancel if payment fails.
   - NETOPIA can move a paid order back to *Failed* and e-mail a new pay link.
4. **NETOPIA refunds run on a plain GET request (no CSRF protection) and can be sent in the wrong currency.**
5. **Eurosite charges stale prices, never syncs booking status, and turns off TLS certificate checks by default.**
6. **FGO may double-count VAT** on stores whose prices include VAT.
7. **The payment add-on is outside CI,** and the database-backed tests never run.

|---|---|---|
| Critical | 2 | — |
| High | 14 | — |
| Medium | 33 | 9 |
| Low / Info | 32 | 15 |

The counts are after merging duplicates; the full list is below. [§9](#9-suggested-roadmap) turns them into a roadmap.

## 1. Fix first: Critical

### C1. Any anonymous visitor can read every guest booking, and rewrite Novoton ones
*Novoton and Sphinx · found independently by two auditors (`novoton-money-1`, `web-security-1`) · confirmed by four verifiers*

- **Where:** `novoton_holidays/src/Repository/BookingOwnershipRepository.php:84, :98`; `sphinx_holidays/src/Repository/SphinxBookingRepository.php:46`; controllers `novoton_booking/edit_booking.php`, `update_booking.php` and `sphinx_booking/edit_booking.php`, `update_booking.php`.
- **What happens:**
  - The check is `booking_id = ? AND (user_id = ? OR session_id = ?)`, called with the visitor's `user_id`.
  - An anonymous visitor has `user_id` 0, and every guest booking is stored with `user_id` 0 (`add_to_cart.php:523-529`, `BookingRecordBuilder.php:76`, Sphinx `CartService.php:329`). So the first branch matches every guest booking.
  - `booking_id` is AUTO_INCREMENT. Stepping through `novoton_booking.edit_booking&booking_id=1,2,3…` shows each traveller's name and date of birth (children included), the hotel, the dates and the price. That is a GDPR-scale personal-data leak.
  - Novoton `update_booking` accepts GET and rewrites names, e-mail, phone and `guests_data`. If the victim's booking is still in their cart, `place_order_post` sends the attacker's names to Novoton (`BookingSubmissionService::hydrateBookingFromDb`).
  - Sphinx is limited to bookings still in a cart (`order_id = 0`). Novoton has no such limit.
  - The 06-24 and 07-01 audits marked this path "no IDOR". The guest case was untested: `BookingOwnershipRepositoryTest` only uses users 42 and 99.
- **Fix:**
  - Match on `user_id` only when it is greater than 0, and on `session_id` only when it is not empty. Apply this in `checkOwnership` and in both `findByIdWithOwnership` methods.
  - Refuse edits once `order_id > 0` or the booking is no longer pending.
  - Make Novoton `update_booking` POST-only.
  - Add tests with `user_id` 0 and a foreign session.
  - Related: `BookingRepository::linkToUserByEmail` (`auth_hooks.php:57/89`) gives every guest booking with a matching e-mail to whoever registers with that e-mail. Require a verified e-mail first.

### C2. Novoton books whatever `rooms_data` the browser sends, but prices only the first room
*Novoton · `novoton-money-3` · confirmed by two verifiers*

- **Where:** `novoton_booking/add_to_cart.php:157-169` (one quote), `:233-264` (price floor), `:345-366`, `:500`, `:545` (raw `rooms_data` stored); `Helpers/BookingRoomAssembler.php:42, :94-168`; `ApiBookingRequestBuilder.php:44, :59-60`.
- **What happens:**
  - `add_to_cart` prices one tuple: the request's room, board and adults, plus the child ages in `guests[]`.
  - It accepts the posted `total_price` when it is at or above that quote.
  - It stores the hidden `rooms_data` JSON almost unchanged. The reservation sent to Novoton is built entirely from `rooms_data`: number of rooms, room and board ids, occupancy and package. `ApiBookingRequestBuilder` even overrides the single-room ids from `rooms_data[0]`.
  - Editing `rooms_data` to two all-inclusive suites while paying the double-room/BB floor books the suites on the agency's account.
- **Mitigation today:** `disable_api_submission` defaults to `Y` (`addon.xml:239`), so the reservation goes out only once production turns API submission on.
- **Fix:** build `rooms_data` on the server from what was priced, or price every room (room, board, package, adults, that room's own child ages) and charge the sum. Reject any request whose rooms, ids or occupancy differ from the verified quote. Also compare Novoton's reservation price with the net price charged.

## 2. Fix first: High

### Money paths that trust request data

**H1. Any order line carrying `extra.travel_balance_id` marks that deposit balance paid** — *travel_core · `core-sphinx-money-1`*
- **Where:** `src/Services/BalanceService.php:46-51, :87-97`; `src/Repository/BalanceRepository.php:134-137` (an unconditional `UPDATE … WHERE balance_id`); `hooks/order_hooks.php:131-157, :174-191`.
- **What happens:** a guest adds any cheap product with `extra[travel_balance_id]=57` and pays it. Balance 57 is set to *paid*, and reminders and overdue alerts stop. Balance ids are sequential, so other customers' balances can be switched off too. The update has no status guard, so it can also turn a *cancelled* balance into *paid*.
- **Depends on:** stock CS-Cart `checkout.add` keeping request-supplied `extra` in the storefront. That is standard core behaviour, but the core is not in the repo to confirm it.
- **Fix:** settle a balance only when the paying line has the balance's own `product_id`, a line total at least the balance amount, and a matching `parent_order_id`. Use `UPDATE … WHERE status='open'`. See also theme [T1](#t1-never-trust-cart-line-extra-from-the-request).

**H2. The Sphinx checkout price check compares `extra.total_price` with the API, never with the price the line charges** — *Sphinx · `core-sphinx-money-2`*
- **Where:** `src/Services/PreOrderPriceVerifier.php:77-84, :149`; `src/Hooks/OrderHooks.php:196-260`.
- **What happens:**
  - Any line with `extra.sphinx_booking` is treated as a Sphinx booking, and `place_order_post` books `extra.offer_id`.
  - A cheap product carrying the extras of an expensive offer is charged its catalogue price while the expensive offer is booked.
  - The check also skips the line entirely when `total_price <= 0`, when a circuit line has no hotel or check-in, when the provider check throws, or when the API is down.
  - The booking is placed at order creation, so no payment is needed at all.
- **Depends on:** the same CS-Cart `extra` behaviour as H1.
- **Fix:** in `pre_place_order`, accept a Sphinx line only if `travel_booking_id` points to a pending row owned by this session or user with the same `offer_id`. Recompute the line price from that row (CurrencyService, then DepositCartLine) and require it to equal `price × amount`.

**H3. A plain Novoton hotel product line is given an orphan booking but keeps the catalogue price** — *Novoton · `novoton-money-2`*
- **Where:** `hooks/cart_hooks.php:60-118, :327-379`; `src/Services/PreOrderPriceVerifier.php:144`.
- **What happens:**
  - The `calculate_cart_items` hook attaches the session's first unused booking to any line for that hotel product that has no booking id.
  - It writes only `extra` and never sets the price or `stored_price`. The line is therefore charged the catalogue "from" price (e.g. €45), and the verifier, which checks `extra.total_price`, sees no problem.
  - Steps: book normally, remove the line, then `checkout.add` the hotel product. The full €1,500 reservation goes to Novoton for €45.
  - Nothing flags the gap: the order page shows the booking total. This one does not depend on CS-Cart keeping request `extra`, because a stock `checkout.add` is enough.
- **Fix:** never attach a booking to a line that `add_to_cart` did not create (or set price and `stored_price='Y'` from the booking when attaching). Refuse `checkout.add` for provider hotel products. Limit `findByProductIds` to `order_id = 0`.

**H4. A Novoton multi-room booking with children is charged one room's price** — *Novoton · `novoton-money-4` · no tampering needed*
- **Where:** `novoton_booking/add_to_cart.php:128-136, :157-166, :227-229`; `booking_form.php:56-59, :118`; `search.tpl:384-393` (both themes).
- **What happens:** the multi-room form posts no adults and no room id. So `add_to_cart` quotes room 1's type for 2 adults plus every child in every room, then sets the cart total to that single quote whenever a child is present.
- **Example:** two rooms worth €1,700 are charged €950, and both rooms are booked. The customer sees it as a "price dropped" message. With more children than room 1 holds, every attempt fails. The deposit is also computed from the wrong total.
- **Fix:** price each room separately (that room's adults, its own child ages, its package). Store the sum and the per-room prices on the server, and have the verifier check each room. Cover `edit_booking` and `update_booking` too.

**H5. Novoton `add_to_cart` reuses another add's pending booking row** — *Novoton (Sphinx has the same pattern) · merged `novoton-money-5`, `web-security-2`, `core-sphinx-money-3`*
- **Where:** `src/Repository/BookingRepository.php:190-209` (`findExisting`); `add_to_cart.php:441-449, :493-519`; Sphinx `CartService.php:252-261`.
- **What happens:**
  - `findExisting` matches hotel, dates and holder name created within the last hour. It does not check session, user, room, board or package.
  - The update branch leaves `package_name`, board, `num_rooms` and `session_id` stale.
  - A customer adding a Double and then a Family room at the same hotel and dates gets two charged lines pointing at one row. Line 1 submits the row (now carrying line 2's rooms), and line 2 is skipped as "already linked". They pay for two rooms and one is booked. The skip is logged only in debug mode.
  - Two strangers with the same holder name within an hour can take over each other's rows. On Sphinx the second room is never booked.
- **Fix:** scope `findExisting` and `findRecentUnassigned` to the session or user, plus room, board, package and occupancy, or drop the reuse. Write every field in the update branch.

### Payments (NETOPIA)

**H6. A NETOPIA IPN can move a paid order backwards and trigger a new pay link** — *`netopia-ipn-1`*
- **Where:** `lib/Ipn/IpnHandler.php:205, :235-259`; `func.php:424, :714`; defaults in `addon.xml:28`.
- **What happens:**
  - The handler skips an IPN only when the new status equals the current one and is P, F or I. Any other non-refund IPN changes the order, including from Paid, Complete, Refunded or Cancelled.
  - Nothing checks that the IPN belongs to the payment attempt that paid.
  - Scenario: the customer pays attempt B, then attempt A is declined in another tab. The paid order becomes *Failed*, `transaction_id` is overwritten (which breaks refunds), an "order failed" e-mail goes out, and the default auto-link hook e-mails a new "pay now" link. A cancel IPN also cancels the customer's open travel balances.
  - Only NETOPIA can send a validly signed IPN, so this is a correctness bug, not an outside attack.
- **Fix:**
  - Store each attempt's `orderID`/`ntpID`, and once the order is P, C or B ignore IPNs from other attempts.
  - Add a transition whitelist that never leaves P, C or B on a non-success IPN; log and alert instead.
  - Move auto-link generation out of the IPN request.

**H7. NETOPIA refunds run on a plain GET, so CS-Cart's CSRF check does not protect them** — *`netopia-admin-2`*
- **Where:** `controllers/backend/netopia_refund.php:35, :50, :127`; `netopia_payment_link.php:31`.
- **What happens:** the refund controller reads `order_id` and `amount` from `$_REQUEST` and never checks the method. CS-Cart validates `security_hash` on POST only. A logged-in admin who follows a link to `admin.php?dispatch=netopia_refund.process&order_id=123&amount=900` sends the customer a 900 RON refund, and it cannot be undone. The config controllers already check for POST; these two do not.
- **Fix:** return early unless `REQUEST_METHOD === 'POST'` in both controllers. The existing forms already POST with `security_hash`.

**H8. The NETOPIA refund panel shows one currency and refunds in another** — *`netopia-admin-1`*
- **Where:** `controllers/backend/orders.post.php:80-114` versus `netopia_refund.php:98-118` and `lib/Payment/RefundFinalizer.php:101-106`.
- **What happens:**
  - The panel's paid, remaining, currency label and "Refund all" value come from `netopia_amount` (the IPN's post-FX amount, e.g. 361.78 RON).
  - The controller caps and sends the refund in `netopia_start_amount` (the start-request currency, e.g. 68.70 EUR).
  - An admin who types 50, meaning RON, refunds 50 EUR, about five times too much. "Refund all" is always rejected on FX orders, and the "remaining" figure is wrong afterwards.
- **Fix:** in `orders.post.php`, read `netopia_start_amount` first and fall back to `netopia_amount`, exactly as the controller does. Add a test for an FX order.

### Invoicing (FGO)

**H9. FGO may double-count VAT when catalogue prices include VAT** — *`fgo-1` · depends on the store's tax setup*
- **Where:** `src/Services/BillingMapper.php:310-313, :327, :430-446`; `src/Dto/Invoice/VatRate.php:60`.
- **What happens:**
  - The mapper always treats the line `subtotal` as net and adds `tax_value` on top.
  - With a CS-Cart tax set to *price includes tax*, `subtotal` is already gross. A 121 RON stay at 21% is then invoiced as 142 RON, and 11% accommodation VAT comes out as 9%.
  - Discount and shipping lines inherit the wrong rate.
  - No check compares the invoice total with the order total ([`fgo-2`](#invoicing-fgo-1)).
  - Stores with no CS-Cart taxes (for example margin-scheme stays with `tax_value=0`) are not affected.
- **Fix:** use `display_subtotal` as the gross, or branch on `price_includes_tax`. Before calling FGO, require the sum of the lines to equal `order.total` within 0.01, and fail clearly otherwise.

### Eurosite (newest provider)

**H10. No live price or availability re-check before the order; the supplier's confirmed price is ignored** — *merged `eurosite-booking-1`, `eurosite-platform-7`*
- **Where:** `func.php:155-195` (`pre_place_order` compares the cart with the booking row written from the same snapshot); `src/Services/OfferContextStore.php` (the snapshot has no timestamp); `src/Api/EurositeApiClient.php:533-558`.
- **What happens:**
  - A €1,000 early-booking offer left in the cart for three weeks is charged €1,000 and booked at Eurosite's current price. The agency absorbs the difference without any alert.
  - A check-in date that has already passed still checks out.
  - Eurosite is the only provider without a `PreOrderPriceVerifier`.
- **Fix:** implement `Contracts\PreOrderPriceVerifierInterface` for Eurosite and apply `CheckoutPriceGuard::policy()`. Refuse past check-ins. Store a snapshot timestamp. Read the price in the AddBooking response and flag mismatches.

**H11. Eurosite booking status never syncs, and every booking that gets a reference is marked *confirmed*** — *`eurosite-booking-2`*
- **Where:** `src/Api/EurositeApiClient.php:579`; `src/Services/BookingSubmissionService.php:171`; `src/Services/BookingStatusService.php:66, :88`.
- **What happens:**
  - The code reads `<Status>`, but the spec's `getBookingResponse` only has `<ItemStatus Code="C|CP|RJ|XP|X">`. The status therefore always reads as empty, and the admin "check status" actions change nothing.
  - Separately, `submitBooking` marks a booking confirmed as soon as any reference comes back, even for on-request offers.
  - The status mapper also matches the text "Confirmation pending" as *confirmed*.
  - A paid stay that EUtour rejects stays "confirmed" until the guest arrives.
- **Fix:** store *pending* after AddBooking, then call `getBooking` (now and from a scheduled job). Map the `ItemStatus` codes exactly (C, CP, RJ, XP, X) and alert the admin on RJ.

**H12. The Eurosite booking request is re-sent on timeout or 5xx, which can create duplicate supplier bookings** — *merged `eurosite-booking-3`, `eurosite-platform-2`; same pattern in Novoton and Sphinx (`data-perf-5`)*
- **Where:** `src/EurositeHttpClient.php:89-119`; `travel_core/src/Http/ResiliencePolicy.php:144-171`; `src/Services/BookingSubmissionService.php:49, :156`.
- **What happens:**
  - `post()` resends every request, `AddBookingRequest` included, on "Operation timed out", "Empty reply", 5xx and 429. Up to 3 POSTs can each create a reservation.
  - If every attempt times out, the row is marked *failed* with no reference, while live bookings may exist at the supplier.
  - Nothing reconciles by the client reference, even though `getBooking(client_ref, 'client')` exists.
  - Novoton (`NovotonHttpClient.php:166, :194`) and Sphinx (`SphinxApi.php:199, :232, :335, :394`) retry their booking calls the same way.
- **Fix:** see theme [T3](#t3-make-every-state-changing-provider-call-idempotent). In short: send booking calls once, retry only connect-phase errors, mark timeouts *unknown*, and reconcile before any resend.

**H13. Eurosite turns off TLS certificate verification by default** — *merged `eurosite-platform-1`, `eurosite-booking-11`*
- **Where:** `addon.xml:103-107` (`allow_insecure_api` ships as `Y`); `src/Services/ConfigProvider.php:136`; `src/EurositeHttpClient.php:47-52, :101-102`.
- **What happens:**
  - The setting is labelled "Allow insecure (HTTP)", but it also sets `CURLOPT_SSL_VERIFYPEER=false` and `VERIFYHOST=0` for https. Scheme-less URLs get `http://`.
  - An attacker on the network path can read the plaintext `RequestUser`/`RequestPass` sent with every request, and rewrite prices or booking references.
  - Novoton, Sphinx, FGO and NETOPIA all keep verification on.
- **Fix:** always verify certificates for https URLs. Let the flag only allow an `http://` scheme, default it to `N`, and fix the tooltip.

## 3. Cross-cutting themes (the improvements that remove whole classes of bugs)

These eight changes would close most of the findings above at the root, instead of one at a time.

### T1. Never trust cart-line `extra` from the request
H1, H2, H3 and C2 all come from one assumption: whatever is in a cart line's `extra` was written by our own server. Proposal:

1. Add a `pre_add_to_cart` hook in travel_core that strips every provider and travel key from request-supplied `product_data[..][extra]`: `travel_*`, `novoton_*`, `sphinx_*`, `eurosite_*`, `total_price`, `rooms_data`, `offer_id`. Only the add-ons' own add-to-cart controllers may set them, since they write the cart directly.
2. Bind each provider line to a server row: `travel_booking_id` must point to a pending row owned by this session or user. Recompute the expected line price from that row in `pre_place_order`, and compare it with `price × amount` — the price actually charged, not `extra.total_price`.
3. Use one dedicated "Balance payment" product for balance lines. This also fixes `core-sphinx-money-6` (Novoton re-pricing balance lines) and `core-sphinx-money-7` (balances unpayable once a hotel product is disabled).

### T2. Book with the supplier only after payment, or cancel when payment fails
All three providers submit the reservation in `place_order_post`. That runs when the order is created, before NETOPIA confirms payment (`novoton-money-10`, `eurosite-booking-10`, and Sphinx per H2). Abandoned or failed payments leave live supplier reservations with cancellation penalties running. It also turns every exploit above into "no payment needed". Proposal: submit on the transition to a paid status (`change_order_status` to P/C). For pay-later methods, keep submission at placement but cancel automatically and alert when the order moves to F, D or I.

### T3. Make every state-changing provider call idempotent
The same mistake appears five times: retrying non-idempotent POSTs on read timeouts and 5xx.
- Eurosite AddBooking (H12)
- the Novoton reservation and Sphinx book calls (`data-perf-5`)
- FGO `factura/emitere` when `verify_duplicate` is off (`fgo-3`)
- FGO storno/cancel (`fgo-4`)
- NETOPIA refund timeouts, recorded as refusals (`netopia-admin-6`)

Proposal:
1. Give `ResiliencePolicy` an explicit "unsafe" mode that retries only connect-phase errors (connection refused, DNS, connect timeout) and 429.
2. Add an *unknown* status for bookings, invoices and refunds.
3. Reconcile *unknown* by client reference (`getBooking(client_ref)`, Novoton `resinfo`, FGO dedup, NETOPIA status) before any resend.
4. Claim the row atomically before the call (`UPDATE … WHERE status='pending'`), as FGO already does for issuing.

### T4. One shared ownership helper
The `user_id = 0` bug (C1) and the unscoped `findExisting` and `findRecentUnassigned` (H5) are the same missing rule, written three ways. Add `TravelCore\Repository\OwnershipScope::sql($userId, $sessionId)`, which never matches `user_id = 0` or an empty session. Use it in every provider repository, and pin it with a test the convergence ledger enforces.

### T5. Admin surface: POST-only mutations and permission schemas that actually gate
- **GET mutations.** Several admin modes still change state on GET:
  - NETOPIA refunds and links (H7);
  - Novoton `run_cron` / `update_prices` / `sync_*` / `recompute_*` / `save_facilities` (`web-security-3`, the 07-01 L2 item, still open);
  - Eurosite `probe_availability` (`eurosite-platform-8`).
- **Permission schemas that gate nothing.** The travel_core, Sphinx and Eurosite schemas use `'permissions' => ['GET','POST']`, a list instead of a method-to-privilege map, so they restrict nothing (`web-security-9`, `eurosite-platform-9`). NETOPIA has no schema at all (`netopia-admin-3`). The `travel_tools`, `travel_alternatives`, `sphinx_holidays` and `sphinx_seo_templates` controllers have no gate of any kind. Any admin can refund, rotate the cron key, or bulk-delete hotels.
- **Proposal:**
  1. Wrap every mutating mode in a POST check.
  2. Use string privileges per mode (FGO's schema is the model to copy).
  3. Add a test that scans `controllers/backend` and fails on any mode without a schema entry.

### T6. A payment state machine for NETOPIA and balances
The order and balance status logic is spread across the IPN handler, the payment-link hook and `BalanceService`. Payments and balances should share one small state machine, with explicit allowed transitions. It needs to:
- bind each IPN to an attempt and check amount and currency against the stored start amount (`netopia-ipn-3`);
- record and alert on a second successful payment (`netopia-ipn-2`);
- refuse payment links for paid orders and never overwrite a paid `transaction_id` (`netopia-admin-4`);
- treat a balance with an active balance order as pending (`core-sphinx-money-5`, `netopia-ipn-5`);
- revert a balance when its order is cancelled or refunded (`core-sphinx-money-10`).

### T7. Currency conversion must fail closed
`CurrencyService` silently uses factor 1.0 for an unknown currency (`core-sphinx-money-12`, `novoton-money-11`, `eurosite-booking-12`), so a €1,000 stay can go into the cart as 1,000 RON. The currency-risk commission is applied in the direction that lowers prices whenever the store's primary currency differs from the API's (`core-sphinx-money-4`). Eurosite re-converts with today's rate and blocks checkout after each daily rate update (`eurosite-booking-8`). Proposal:
1. Refuse to add to the cart when a coefficient is missing.
2. Apply the risk margin explicitly in `convertFromApiCurrency`.
3. Compare prices in the API currency, not by re-converting.
4. Add a RON-primary test fixture.

### T8. CI should gate everything that ships
- NETOPIA into root CI, and the database job turned on (`netopia-admin-8`, `data-perf-8`).
- One add-on registry so a new add-on cannot silently miss a gate (A1 in [§5](#5-architecture-and-maintainability)); `composer test` already skips Eurosite.
- Novoton has no post-install schema heal (`data-perf-3`) and travel_core misses one table (`data-perf-2`). A "fresh install == upgraded install" schema test against a real database would catch both classes.

### Medium

**A1. The add-on list is maintained by hand in about 8 places, and it has already drifted** (`arch-repo-3`)

## 7. Checked and found sound

So the next audit does not redo these:

- **NETOPIA IPN authenticity.** RS256/384/512 only, so neither `none` nor HS* is accepted. The issuer is pinned, `aud` is the POS signature (checked with `hash_equals`), and `sub` is the hash of the exact raw body. Size caps apply. Every write happens only after verification.
- **NETOPIA order binding.** The `orderID` is server-made and signed. The hosted return changes no status (HMAC-checked `order_id`). 3DS takes the order from the session only. No card data is handled, and the API key is never logged. TLS is verified with a 30 s timeout.
- **FGO.**
  - Invoice claiming is atomic: `UNIQUE` + `INSERT IGNORE` and conditional `UPDATE`.
  - CIF and CNP checksums were verified with real values; the CNP is masked everywhere.
  - Request signing matches the reference plugins.
  - The PDF fetcher's SSRF guard allows https, `*.fgo.ro` and port 443 only, with redirects re-validated.
  - Every admin mode is POST-only with a complete permission schema.
- **Eurosite.**
  - Prices, rooms, board and dates come from the server-side snapshot and cannot be changed by POST.
  - Request XML values are escaped with `ENT_XML1`, and parsing uses `LIBXML_NONET` without `NOENT`.
  - It uses `ResiliencePolicy` and `CronRunLock`.
  - The conversion direction matches CS-Cart.
- **Novoton checkout.**
  - `pre_place_order` and `place_order_post` signatures match CS-Cart, including both 4.20 argument orders.
  - The linked-order duplicate guard works.
  - Deposits are computed from the booked offer's terms on the server.
  - `HotelCartProduct` accepts only the hotel's own active products.
- **Cron endpoints.** All use `hash_equals` and refuse an empty key, and modes are allow-listed.
- **Packaging and logging.** `scripts/package-addons.php` never ships `dev/`, `tests/`, HAR files or vendor. Provider logs go to the database, not to web-readable files.
- **Data layer.**
  - Indexes match the WHERE clauses on the request paths.
  - No N+1 queries were found on listing pages.
  - Sphinx and Eurosite schema migrators are idempotent and self-healing.
  - Caches have expiry and cleanup.

## 10. What to confirm on the live store

Some findings depend on things outside this repository:

1. **CS-Cart core:** does storefront `checkout.add` keep request-supplied `product_data[..][extra]`? This decides whether H1 and H2 are exploitable as described. Test it on a staging store with a crafted POST.
2. **Store tax setup:** is there a CS-Cart tax with *price includes tax* on hotel products, and with which calculation method? This decides H9.
3. **Supplier de-duplication:** do Eurosite (`BookingClientId`), Novoton (`OrderNum`) and Sphinx de-duplicate repeated booking requests? If they do, H12 drops to Medium, but the orphaned and unknown bookings remain.
4. **NETOPIA:**
   - Which IPN statuses are actually sent (pending, 15, expired)? This affects H6 and `netopia-ipn-4`.
   - How long do hosted pay links stay payable?
   - Does the IPN reply's `errorType` follow the SDK convention (0 = none, 1 = temporary, 2 = permanent)? If it does, the add-on's OK replies (errorType=1) would be read as temporary errors.
5. **Novoton API submission:** `disable_api_submission` defaults to `Y`. Is it on in production? This decides how urgent C2 and H4 are.
6. **Web server:** is `app/` (cron scripts, NETOPIA key folder) and `var/` denied on nginx as well as Apache?
